Snazzy Paws Grooming

About Us

Snazzy Paws Grooming is a friendly, family-run dog grooming salon, based in Hamilton. We treat every dog like one of our own and take the time to keep them calm and comfortable.

From a quick bath and brush to a full pamper session, we make sure your dog leaves clean, healthy and looking snazzy.

Popular Services

  • Bath and Brush

    Shampoo, blow-dry, brush-out and nail trim.

  • Haircut

    Bath, blow-dry, breed or custom haircut and nail trim.

  • Puppy Package

    A gentle introduction to grooming for puppies.

  • Spa Package

    Bath, haircut, teeth brushing, nail trim and special scents.

About the Owners

Snazzy Paws was started by two lifelong dog lovers who wanted a grooming salon where dogs feel safe and owners feel listened to.

Between them they have over 15 years of grooming experience, from tiny puppies to senior dogs who need a little extra care.
